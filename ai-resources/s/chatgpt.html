ChatGPT — what it is actually for

ChatGPT is OpenAI's assistant app for web, desktop, and mobile. One subscription covers text chat, image generation, voice, deep research, projects, and a limited or expanded amount of Codex coding work.

Short answer: use ChatGPT when you need drafting and editing text in a chat interface. It is a free tier + subscription service from OpenAI; the official pricing source is linked below.

What ChatGPT is used for

  • Drafting and editing text in a chat interface
  • Running deep-research reports that cite web sources
  • Generating and editing images from prompts

Source: official product page and official documentation.

Who ChatGPT is for

  • General knowledge work, writing, and research
  • Teams that want one assistant for text, images, voice, and research
  • Users who also want access to Codex for coding

Source: official documentation.

Who it is not for

  • Workloads that need a fixed per-token budget without a subscription
  • Deployments that require a self-hosted model

Source: documented plans, limits, and deployment model on the official product page.

ChatGPT FAQ

Is ChatGPT free?

Yes. ChatGPT has a Free plan with limited messages and features. Paid plans add higher limits, advanced reasoning, expanded research, and more Codex usage.

Is ChatGPT the same as the OpenAI API?

No. ChatGPT is the consumer and business app. The OpenAI API is a separate, usage-based developer service billed per token, image, or audio unit.

What can I use instead of ChatGPT?

Alternatives in this directory include Claude, Gemini, and Mistral Le Chat. Each has a different pricing model and model family.
