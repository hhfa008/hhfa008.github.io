Pika — what it is actually for

Pika generates short videos and images from prompts and reference images through pika.art. Plans are metered in credits, and users can buy one-time top-up credits in addition to a subscription.

Short answer: use Pika when you need social short videos and effects. It is a free tier + subscription + credit top-ups service from Pika; the official pricing source is linked below.

What Pika is used for

  • Social short videos and effects
  • Image-to-video animation
  • Fast iteration on short clips

Source: official product page and official documentation.

Who Pika is for

  • Creators making social short videos
  • Users who want a free tier and optional top-ups
  • Teams that need a commercial license on paid plans

Source: official documentation.

Who it is not for

  • Long-form production workflows
  • Buyers who need per-second API pricing only

Source: documented plans, limits, and deployment model on the official product page.

Pricing

  • Free plan with limited credits; paid Starter, Creator (US$35/month), and Fancy plans include monthly credit allowances.Source: pika.art
  • Top-up credits cost US$1 per 60 credits and can be purchased without changing plans.Source: pika.art
  • Credit cost per generation depends on the model and duration. For example, Seedance 2.5 at 720p for 5 seconds costs 122 credits.Source: pika.art

Top-up credits are limited to Pika Create on pika.art and MCP on pika.art/mcp; they cannot be used with the API, iOS app, or old.pika.art.

Full pricing page: https://pika.art/pricing

Pika FAQ

Is Pika free?

Pika lists a Free plan with limited credits. Paid plans add monthly credits and commercial use.

Can I buy Pika credits without a subscription?

Yes. Pika sells one-time top-up credits at US$1 per 60 credits, with restrictions on where they can be used.

How many credits does a Pika video cost?

It depends on the model and duration. Pika's pricing page lists 122 credits for a 5-second Seedance 2.5 clip at 720p.
