Black Forest Labs FLUX — what it is actually for

Black Forest Labs develops the FLUX model family. It sells pay-as-you-go API access with no subscription and offers commercial licenses for self-hosting and fine-tuning.

Short answer: use Black Forest Labs FLUX when you need per-image generation in an application. It is a usage-based api + licensing service from Black Forest Labs; the official pricing source is linked below.

What Black Forest Labs FLUX is used for

  • Per-image generation in an application

Who Black Forest Labs FLUX is for

  • Developers who want per-image API pricing
  • Teams that need fine-tuning or LoRA rights
  • Organizations that want to self-host FLUX models

Source: official documentation.

Who it is not for

  • Consumers who want a finished design app
  • Buyers who need a free production tier

Source: documented plans, limits, and deployment model on the official product page.

Pricing

Black Forest Labs FLUX FAQ

Is FLUX free?

There is no published free API tier. Some FLUX models have open weights, but commercial self-hosting requires the applicable license.

How much does the FLUX API cost?

Pricing is pay-as-you-go and depends on the model, resolution, and number of images. The official calculator shows a sample rate of US$0.048 per image for FLUX 3 Image at 768x768.

Can I fine-tune FLUX?

Yes. Black Forest Labs sells licenses that include fine-tuning and LoRA rights on its builder, platform, and professional tiers.
