Google Veo — what it is actually for

Veo is Google DeepMind's video generation model. Consumers reach it through Google AI subscription plans and Google's creative tools; developers use it through Vertex AI and the Gemini API with per-second pricing.

Short answer: use Google Veo when you need text-to-video and image-to-video with audio. It is a subscription access + usage-based api service from Google DeepMind; the official pricing source is linked below.

What Google Veo is used for

  • Text-to-video and image-to-video with audio
  • Advertising and creative prototyping
  • Video generation inside developer pipelines

Source: official product page and official documentation.

Who Google Veo is for

  • Teams already using Google Cloud or Gemini
  • Developers who need an API-first video model
  • Creators on a Google AI subscription

Source: official documentation.

Who it is not for

  • Buyers who need a fixed monthly video quota
  • Deployments that require offline generation

Source: documented plans, limits, and deployment model on the official product page.

Pricing

  • Developer use is billed per second of generated video, with Standard, Fast, and Lite model tiers.Source: cloud.google.com
  • Rates depend on model tier, resolution, and platform. The official pricing page lists the current per-second table.Source: cloud.google.com
  • Consumer access is bundled into Google AI plans, which have their own monthly prices and usage limits.Source: gemini.google

Veo rates differ between the Gemini API and Vertex AI, and between resolutions. The official Google Cloud pricing page is authoritative.

Full pricing page: https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/pricing

Google Veo FAQ

Is Veo free?

No free API tier is published. Consumers can access Veo through Google AI plans, which have monthly subscription prices and usage limits.

How is Veo billed for developers?

Google bills per second of generated video. Rates vary by model tier, resolution, and whether you use Vertex AI or the Gemini API.

Does Veo generate audio?

Google documents Veo as generating video with audio. Check the current model documentation for supported outputs and limits.
