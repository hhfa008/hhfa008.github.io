Arize Phoenix — what it is actually for

Arize Phoenix is an open-source observability and evaluation tool for LLM applications. It supports local tracing, experiments, and evaluation, with Phoenix Cloud and enterprise options.

Short answer: use Arize Phoenix when you need local tracing and debugging of llm apps. It is a open source + free cloud tier + subscription service from Arize AI; the official pricing source is linked below.

What Arize Phoenix is used for

  • Local tracing and debugging of LLM apps
  • Evaluation experiments and RAG analysis
  • OpenTelemetry-based instrumentation

Source: official product page and official documentation.

Who Arize Phoenix is for

  • Teams that want open-source plus OpenTelemetry
  • Local debugging and RAG evaluation
  • Cost-sensitive evaluation workflows

Source: official documentation.

Who it is not for

  • Teams that need a fully managed enterprise suite from day one
  • No-code users

Source: documented plans, limits, and deployment model on the official product page.

Pricing

  • The open-source project is free to self-host.Source: phoenix.arize.com
  • Phoenix Cloud free tier includes 25,000 spans per month, 10 issues, and 1 GB of data.Source: phoenix.arize.com
  • The paid cloud tier is US$50 per month with 50,000 spans, 25 issues, and 10 GB; Enterprise pricing is custom.Source: phoenix.arize.com

The self-hosted open-source edition has no license fee. Cloud tiers meter spans, issues, and stored data.

Full pricing page: https://phoenix.arize.com/pricing/

Arize Phoenix FAQ

Is Arize Phoenix free?

Yes. The open-source edition is free to self-host, and Phoenix Cloud has a free tier with 25,000 spans per month.

Does Phoenix support OpenTelemetry?

Yes. Phoenix documents OpenTelemetry-based instrumentation for LLM applications.

What is included in the paid Phoenix tier?

The US$50/month tier includes 50,000 spans, 25 issues, and 10 GB of data according to the pricing page.
