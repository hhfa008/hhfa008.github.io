Luma Dream Machine — what it is actually for

Luma Dream Machine is a creative agent that generates video and images using Luma and third-party models. Individual plans include monthly credits, and team plans add shared credits and usage analytics.

Short answer: use Luma Dream Machine when you need text-to-video and image-to-video generation. It is a free trial + subscription service from Luma AI; the official pricing source is linked below.

What Luma Dream Machine is used for

  • Text-to-video and image-to-video generation
  • Creative exploration across multiple models
  • Team production with shared credits

Source: official product page and official documentation.

Who Luma Dream Machine is for

  • Creators who want several models in one interface
  • Teams that need shared credit pools
  • Users producing short video and image assets

Source: official documentation.

Who it is not for

  • Users who need a permanent free tier
  • Buyers who need per-second API budgeting on a subscription

Source: documented plans, limits, and deployment model on the official product page.

Pricing

  • Plus at US$30/month (US$25/month billed annually) with 10,000 credits.Source: lumalabs.ai
  • Pro at US$90/month (US$75 annual) with 40,000 credits; Ultra at US$300/month (US$250 annual) with 150,000 credits.Source: lumalabs.ai
  • Team plans are quoted by Luma and add shared credits, member management, and usage analytics.Source: lumalabs.ai

Plans include access to Luma and third-party image and video models. Commercial use is included on paid individual plans.

Full pricing page: https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine

Luma Dream Machine FAQ

Is Luma Dream Machine free?

Luma offers a free trial. Ongoing use requires a paid Plus, Pro, Ultra, or Team plan.

What models are available in Dream Machine?

Luma's pricing page lists Luma and third-party image and video models, including GPT Image, Seedream, Veo, Kling, and ElevenLabs models.

Do Luma plans include commercial use?

Yes. Luma lists commercial use as included on its paid individual plans.
