Adobe Firefly — what it is actually for

Adobe Firefly generates and edits images, video, and audio inside Adobe Creative Cloud apps and the Firefly web app. Paid plans combine monthly generative credits with unlimited standard image and vector generations.

Short answer: use Adobe Firefly when you need commercial design work inside photoshop and express. It is a free plan + subscription service from Adobe; the official pricing source is linked below.

Who Adobe Firefly is for

  • Adobe Creative Cloud users

Who it is not for

  • Users who do not want an Adobe subscription

Adobe Firefly FAQ

Is Adobe Firefly free?

Adobe offers a free Firefly plan with a limited generative-credit allowance. Paid plans add larger monthly credit allowances and premium features.

How many credits do Firefly plans include?

Adobe's FAQ lists 2,000 credits for Standard, 4,000 for Pro, and 50,000 for Premium. Team plans have their own allowances.

Do Firefly credits roll over?

No. Adobe states that credits reset monthly and unused credits do not roll over.
