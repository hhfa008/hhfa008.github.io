Runway — what it is actually for

Runway provides video and image generation models through a web app and API. Subscriptions include monthly credits, and each generation costs a different number of credits depending on the model and resolution.

Short answer: use Runway when you need text-to-video and image-to-video clips. It is a free tier + subscription service from Runway; the official pricing source is linked below.

What Runway is used for

  • Text-to-video and image-to-video clips
  • Storyboards, VFX, and visual prototyping
  • Programmatic generation through the API

Source: official product page and official documentation.

Who Runway is for

  • Creators and studios producing short video
  • Teams that want video and image generation in one tool
  • Developers who need a video API

Source: official documentation.

Who it is not for

  • Users who need an ongoing free quota
  • Buyers who need a fixed per-second price on subscription plans

Source: documented plans, limits, and deployment model on the official product page.

Runway FAQ

Is Runway free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 125 one-time credits. Continued use requires a paid plan or credit top-up.

How are Runway credits calculated?

Each generation costs credits based on the model, resolution, and duration. The official pricing page lists example credit costs.

Does Runway have an API?

Yes. Runway publishes API documentation for programmatic video and image generation.
