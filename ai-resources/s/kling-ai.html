Kling AI — what it is actually for

Kling AI generates videos and images from text or reference images. It offers a free daily credit allowance, paid subscription tiers, and a separate developer API.

Short answer: use Kling AI when you need short video generation for social media. It is a free daily credits + subscription + usage-based api service from Kuaishou; the official pricing source is linked below.

Kling AI FAQ

Is Kling AI free?

Kling offers a free daily credit allowance. Free output is watermarked and restricted to non-commercial use; paid tiers remove these limits.

Can I use Kling commercially for free?

No. Kling's free tier is non-commercial. Commercial use requires a paid tier or API plan.

Are Kling subscription credits the same as API credits?

No. Consumer membership credits and developer API units are separate products with separate pricing.
