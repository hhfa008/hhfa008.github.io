Braintrust — what it is actually for

Braintrust provides evaluations, tracing and logging, datasets, a prompt playground, and production monitoring for AI applications. It has a free tier and a flat-fee Pro plan.

Short answer: use Braintrust when you need building eval suites for llm features. It is a free tier + subscription + usage service from Braintrust; the official pricing source is linked below.

Free option

What Braintrust is used for

  • Building eval suites for LLM features
  • Comparing prompts and model versions
  • Production logging and regression testing

Source: official product page and official documentation.

Who Braintrust is for

  • Product teams shipping LLM features
  • Eval-first development workflows
  • Cross-provider model comparisons

Source: official documentation.

Who it is not for

  • Teams that need a free self-hosted option
  • Projects that only need tracing

Source: documented plans, limits, and deployment model on the official product page.

Pricing

  • Free tier with included usage and model credits.Source: www.braintrust.dev
  • Pro is US$249 per month and includes US$100 in model credits; Enterprise pricing is custom.Source: www.braintrust.dev
  • Storage and eval usage beyond the included amounts are metered, such as per GB and per 1,000 evaluations.Source: www.braintrust.dev

The Pro plan bundles model credits with the platform fee; usage above the included amounts is billed separately.

Full pricing page: https://www.braintrust.dev/pricing

Alternatives to Braintrust

  • Arize Phoenix — Open-source LLM tracing and evaluation, with a managed cloud.
  • W&B Weave — Tracing and evaluation for LLM apps inside the Weights & Biases platform.

Braintrust FAQ

Is Braintrust free?

Yes. Braintrust has a free tier with no credit card required, including model credits and included usage.

How much does Braintrust Pro cost?

Pro is US$249 per month and includes US$100 in model credits. Additional storage and eval usage is metered.

What is Braintrust used for?

It is used for LLM evaluation, tracing, prompt experimentation, and production monitoring.
