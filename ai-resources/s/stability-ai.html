Stability AI — what it is actually for

Stability AI offers hosted APIs for image generation, editing, upscaling, audio, and 3D assets, priced in credits. It also publishes open-weight Stable Diffusion models that can be self-hosted under their licenses.

Short answer: use Stability AI when you need per-image cost-controlled generation. It is a free credits + usage-based api service from Stability AI; the official pricing source is linked below.

What Stability AI is used for

  • Per-image cost-controlled generation
  • Upscaling, inpainting, background removal, and relighting
  • Self-hosting Stable Diffusion models

Source: official product page and official documentation.

Who Stability AI is for

  • Developers who want per-image cost control
  • Teams that need upscaling and editing endpoints
  • Organizations that want to self-host open-weight models

Source: official documentation.

Who it is not for

  • Buyers who want a polished consumer app
  • Teams that require parity with the largest closed image models

Source: documented plans, limits, and deployment model on the official product page.

Pricing

  • API usage is credit-based. One credit equals US$0.01, and new accounts receive 25 free credits.Source: platform.stability.ai
  • Stable Image Core costs 3 credits (about US$0.03); Stable Image Ultra costs 8 credits (about US$0.08); SD 3.5 Large costs 6.5 credits.Source: platform.stability.ai
  • Upscalers, editing operations, audio, and 3D services have their own credit prices. Self-hosting uses separate license terms.Source: platform.stability.ai

Credit prices are published on the official pricing page and can change. Open-weight self-hosting is not billed by the API.

Full pricing page: https://platform.stability.ai/pricing

Alternatives to Stability AI

  • OpenAI Image API — Image generation and editing API, also available inside ChatGPT.

Stability AI FAQ

Is Stability AI free?

There is no free production tier, but new API accounts receive 25 free credits. After that, usage is billed in credits at US$0.01 each.

Can Stability models be self-hosted?

Yes. Stability publishes open-weight Stable Diffusion models that can be self-hosted under their license terms.

What does one Stability credit cost?

One credit equals US$0.01 according to the official API pricing page.
