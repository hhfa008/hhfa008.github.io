Gemini — what it is actually for

Gemini is Google's assistant family. The consumer app covers chat, image generation, Deep Research, Gemini Live, Canvas, Gems, and Google app integrations. Developers access Gemini models through the Gemini API and Vertex AI.

Short answer: use Gemini when you need assistant work inside google workspace and chrome. It is a free tier + subscription + usage-based api service from Google; the official pricing source is linked below.

Free option

What Gemini is used for

  • Assistant work inside Google Workspace and Chrome
  • Multimodal prompts with images, audio, and video
  • Building with Gemini models through the Gemini API

Source: official product page and official documentation.

Who Gemini is for

  • People already working inside Google Workspace
  • Multimodal work that combines text, images, audio, and video
  • Developers who want low-cost Flash models through an API

Source: official documentation.

Who it is not for

  • Users who need the Pro model on the free tier
  • Teams that require a self-hosted assistant

Source: documented plans, limits, and deployment model on the official product page.

Pricing

  • Free plan with access to the Flash-Lite model and limited usage of research and media features.Source: gemini.google
  • Google AI Plus, AI Pro, and AI Ultra subscription tiers. AI Ultra is listed at US$99.99/month for 5x and US$199.99/month for 20x the AI Pro limits.Source: gemini.google
  • Gemini API is billed per token. The free API tier is limited to Flash and Flash-Lite models.Source: ai.google.dev

Plan availability and prices vary by country. The official subscription page shows the local price.

Full pricing page: https://gemini.google/subscriptions/

Gemini FAQ

Is Gemini free?

Yes. The Free plan provides Flash-Lite and limited features. Flash and Pro models require a paid Google AI plan.

What is Google AI Ultra?

It is Google's highest consumer AI tier, listed at US$99.99/month for 5x and US$199.99/month for 20x the AI Pro usage limits.

Can developers use Gemini through an API?

Yes. The Gemini API is billed per token, with a free tier limited to Flash and Flash-Lite models.
