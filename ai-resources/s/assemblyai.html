AssemblyAI — what it is actually for

AssemblyAI provides pre-recorded and streaming speech-to-text, audio intelligence features, and a voice agent API. Pricing is pay-as-you-go per audio hour, with add-ons priced per hour.

Short answer: use AssemblyAI when you need transcription with speaker diarization. It is a free credits + usage-based api service from AssemblyAI; the official pricing source is linked below.

What AssemblyAI is used for

  • Transcription with speaker diarization
  • PII redaction and audio intelligence
  • Voice agents that combine speech-to-text, LLM, and TTS

Source: official product page and official documentation.

Who AssemblyAI is for

  • Developers who need transcription plus audio intelligence
  • High-concurrency transcription workloads
  • Teams that want predictable per-hour costs

Source: official documentation.

Who it is not for

  • Users who want a consumer transcription app
  • Buyers who need unlimited free usage

Source: documented plans, limits, and deployment model on the official product page.

Pricing

  • US$50 in free credits, then pay-as-you-go with no minimum commitment.Source: www.assemblyai.com
  • Universal-2 asynchronous speech-to-text is listed at US$0.15/hour; streaming is listed at US$0.15/hour.Source: www.assemblyai.com
  • The Voice Agent API is listed at US$4.50/hour (US$0.075/minute). Optional features such as diarization and PII redaction are priced per hour.Source: www.assemblyai.com

Pre-recorded audio is billed per audio hour submitted; multichannel audio is billed per channel.

Full pricing page: https://www.assemblyai.com/pricing

Alternatives to AssemblyAI

  • Deepgram — Speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and voice agent APIs with per-minute billing.
  • OpenAI Audio API — Speech-to-text and text-to-speech APIs billed by usage.
  • Azure AI Speech — Enterprise speech-to-text, neural TTS, translation, and speaker recognition.

AssemblyAI FAQ

Is AssemblyAI free?

New accounts receive US$50 in free credits. After that, usage is billed pay-as-you-go.

How much does AssemblyAI transcription cost?

The official pricing page lists Universal-2 asynchronous speech-to-text at US$0.15/hour and streaming at US$0.15/hour.

Does AssemblyAI include diarization?

Speaker diarization is available, with per-hour add-on pricing for asynchronous audio according to the pricing page.
