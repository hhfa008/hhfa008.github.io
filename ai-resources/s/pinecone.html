Pinecone — what it is actually for

Pinecone is a managed vector database with serverless indexes, metadata filtering, hybrid search, and an Assistant for RAG. It offers a free Starter plan and usage-based paid plans.

Short answer: use Pinecone when you need production vector search. It is a free tier + subscription + usage-based service from Pinecone; the official pricing source is linked below.

Pricing

  • Starter is free. Builder is US$20/month flat. Standard has a US$50/month minimum with usage-based charges above it.Source: www.pinecone.io
  • Enterprise has a US$500/month minimum with usage-based charges above it.Source: www.pinecone.io
  • Storage, read units, write units, and egress are metered. The Standard trial includes US$300 in credits.Source: www.pinecone.io

The Standard and Enterprise minimums are floors applied to usage, not add-on fees. The official pricing page lists current unit rates.

Full pricing page: https://www.pinecone.io/pricing/

Pinecone FAQ

Is Pinecone free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free with storage and usage limits. Paid plans start with Builder at US$20/month.

What is the Pinecone Standard minimum?

Standard has a US$50/month minimum. Usage above the minimum is billed pay-as-you-go.

Can Pinecone be self-hosted?

Pinecone is a managed service. Self-hosting is not part of its published plans.
