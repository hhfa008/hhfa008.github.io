W&B Weave — what it is actually for

W&B Weave provides tracing, evaluation, and monitoring for LLM applications and integrates with Weights & Biases experiment tracking. It is available on the W&B Free, Pro, and Enterprise plans.

Short answer: use W&B Weave when you need llm tracing and evaluation. It is a free tier + subscription service from Weights & Biases (CoreWeave); the official pricing source is linked below.

What W&B Weave is used for

  • LLM tracing and evaluation
  • Comparing model and prompt versions
  • Team dashboards alongside experiment tracking

Source: official product page and official documentation.

Who W&B Weave is for

  • Teams already using Weights & Biases
  • ML teams that want evals and experiment tracking together
  • Organizations that need enterprise controls

Source: official documentation.

Who it is not for

  • Teams that want lightweight standalone tracing
  • Buyers who need a free self-hosted edition

Source: documented plans, limits, and deployment model on the official product page.

Pricing

  • Free plan for personal development, including AI application evaluations, tracing, and scorers.Source: wandb.ai
  • Pro starts at US$60 per month with a 30-day free trial and adds unlimited teams, access controls, and automations.Source: wandb.ai
  • Enterprise pricing is custom. Academic research has a separate free program.Source: wandb.ai

W&B Weave FAQ

Is W&B Weave free?

Yes. The W&B Free plan includes AI application tracing, evaluations, and scorers.

How much does W&B Pro cost?

Pro starts at US$60 per month and includes a 30-day free trial.

Does Weave replace experiment tracking?

No. Weave is the LLM application tracing and evaluation layer inside the broader Weights & Biases platform.
