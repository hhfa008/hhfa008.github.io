Midjourney — what it is actually for

Midjourney generates images and short videos from text prompts. Plans are metered by GPU time rather than a fixed image count, and generations run through the Midjourney web app or Discord.

Short answer: use Midjourney when you need concept art and stylized illustration. It is a subscription only service from Midjourney; the official pricing source is linked below.

What Midjourney is used for

  • Concept art and stylized illustration
  • Iterating on visual styles with variations
  • Short video generation on higher plans

Source: official product page and official documentation.

Who Midjourney is for

  • Illustrators and designers who want a subscription tool
  • Users who need private generations on Pro or Mega
  • Teams producing stylized image sets

Source: official documentation.

Who it is not for

  • Users who need a free tier
  • Developers who need an official public API

Source: documented plans, limits, and deployment model on the official product page.

Pricing

  • Basic at US$10/month, Standard at US$30/month, Pro at US$60/month, and Mega at US$120/month.Source: www.midjourney.com
  • Annual billing is about 20% cheaper. Plans are metered by GPU time, not by a fixed image count.Source: www.midjourney.com
  • Extra Fast hours cost US$4/hour. Stealth mode is available on Pro and Mega, and companies above the revenue threshold in the terms must use Pro or Mega.Source: www.midjourney.com

Midjourney does not publish a public API. Third-party integrations are not official.

Full pricing page: https://www.midjourney.com/plans

Alternatives to Midjourney

  • OpenAI Image API — Image generation and editing API, also available inside ChatGPT.
  • Stability AI — Credit-based API for Stable Image, audio, and 3D models, plus open weights.
  • Black Forest Labs FLUX — FLUX image and video models through a pay-as-you-go API or self-hosted weights.

Midjourney FAQ

Is Midjourney free?

No. Midjourney does not publish a free tier or free trial. The lowest paid plan is Basic at US$10/month.

Does Midjourney have an API?

No official public API is published. Midjourney is used through its web app and Discord.

What is the difference between Midjourney plans?

Higher plans add more Fast GPU hours, Relax mode, video generation, and Stealth mode. The official plans page lists the current limits.
