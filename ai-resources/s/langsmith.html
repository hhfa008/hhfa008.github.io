LangSmith — what it is actually for

LangSmith provides tracing, datasets, evaluators, annotation queues, prompt management, and deployment for LLM applications. It offers a free Developer tier and a per-seat Plus plan.

Short answer: use LangSmith when you need debugging agent and chain traces. It is a free tier + per-seat subscription + usage service from LangChain; the official pricing source is linked below.

What LangSmith is used for

  • Debugging agent and chain traces
  • Offline and online evaluations
  • Prompt iteration and dataset management

Source: official product page and official documentation.

Who LangSmith is for

  • Teams building and operating agents
  • LangChain and LangGraph users
  • Eval-driven development workflows

Source: official documentation.

Who it is not for

  • Teams that need a fully self-hosted free product
  • Projects that only need basic log storage

Source: documented plans, limits, and deployment model on the official product page.

Pricing

  • Developer is free with 1 seat and 5,000 base traces per month.Source: www.langchain.com
  • Plus is US$39 per seat per month with 10,000 base traces per month; Enterprise pricing is custom.Source: www.langchain.com
  • Usage beyond the included traces is billed. One LangChain Standard Unit (LSU) is priced at US$1.Source: www.langchain.com

Base traces have shorter retention; longer retention and advanced features consume extended-retention or other metered units.

Full pricing page: https://www.langchain.com/pricing

Alternatives to LangSmith
