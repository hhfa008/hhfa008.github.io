Deepgram — what it is actually for

Deepgram provides speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and voice agent APIs. Usage is billed per minute for speech-to-text and per 1,000 characters for text-to-speech, with a free starting credit.

Short answer: use Deepgram when you need call and meeting transcription. It is a free credit + usage-based api service from Deepgram; the official pricing source is linked below.

Who Deepgram is for

Who it is not for

Pricing

  • US$200 free credit, then pay-as-you-go with no minimum commitment.Source: deepgram.com
  • Pre-recorded Nova-3 speech-to-text is listed from US$0.0043/minute for monolingual audio; streaming is listed at US$0.0077/minute regular, with promotional rates.Source: deepgram.com
  • Text-to-speech is priced per 1,000 characters (for example, Aura-2 at US$0.030/1k characters). Growth and Enterprise plans offer prepaid credits and volume terms.Source: deepgram.com

Billing is per second with no rounding up to the minute. Multichannel audio is billed per channel.

Full pricing page: https://deepgram.com/pricing

Deepgram FAQ

Is Deepgram free?

New accounts receive a US$200 free credit. After that, usage is billed pay-as-you-go.

How is Deepgram billed?

Speech-to-text is billed per minute and text-to-speech per 1,000 characters. Billing is per second without rounding up to the minute.

Does Deepgram offer a voice agent API?

Yes. Deepgram lists a Voice Agent API alongside its speech-to-text and text-to-speech products.
