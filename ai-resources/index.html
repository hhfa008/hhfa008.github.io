How to read the pricing labels

The labels are deliberately simple. They describe how a provider publishes pricing, not which option is cheaper for a particular workload.

Free tier

A published free plan, free credits, or a free open-source edition. Limits and commercial-use terms vary; each service page links to the official terms.

Subscription

A recurring per-user, per-seat, or per-workspace fee. Usage may still be capped by credits, messages, or traces.

Usage-based

Billing per token, minute, image, second, trace, or request. The service page links to the provider's published unit prices.

No price is inferred. If a provider does not publish a number, the entry says so and links to the page that would contain it.

Questions about this directory

What is this AI services directory?

It is an independent directory of 40 real AI services across eight categories. Each entry states what the service does, who it fits, its published pricing model, and links to official pricing and documentation.

Does the directory include ratings or user numbers?

No. It does not publish subjective scores, user counts, funding figures, or invented benchmarks. It records public facts and links to primary sources.

How is pricing described?

With three labels: free tier, subscription, and usage-based. Exact prices appear only when the provider publishes them on an official pricing page.
