Terms and boundaries

What the fee buys — and what it does not promise.

Included in every fixed-scope engagement: one written report, one 60-minute recorded walkthrough, and one revision to the report and a re-check of the same scope. The report separates what changed, what did not, and what remains uncertain. A mutual NDA is available on request. Quotes are valid for 30 days.

Not promised: App Store approval, a specific launch time, or a specific percentage improvement in any metric. I stand behind the method, the measurements, and the written deliverables — not a guaranteed outcome.
