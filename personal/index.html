FAQ

The questions that come up before a first email.

What access do you need?

Read-only repository access or a buildable archive, plus a build or TestFlight link that reproduces the problem. For a performance audit I also need the flow, the device class, and the approximate time when the symptom appears.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. A mutual NDA is available on request and is signed before repository access is granted.

What is in the report?

A written report in PDF and Markdown, the prioritized backlog, the traces and measurements behind each finding, and a 60-minute walkthrough. The report states the baseline and the measurement definition so another engineer can reproduce the numbers.

How many revisions are included?

One revision per fixed-scope engagement. A revision covers corrections to the report and a re-check of the same scope, not a new app or a new flow.

How does payment work?

50% at kickoff and 50% on delivery for fixed-scope work. The ongoing contract is billed weekly at $50 per hour with a 15-hour weekly minimum.

How long is a quote valid?

30 days from the date of the quote. If the scope changes, the quote is reissued before work continues.

Do you guarantee a performance outcome?

No. I guarantee the measurement method, the written deliverables, and the revision. The report separates what changed, what did not change, and what remains uncertain.

How is the ongoing contract different from an audit?

An audit is a fixed-scope, fixed-price deliverable that ends with a report. The ongoing contract is embedded work at $50 per hour, minimum 15 hours per week, for teams that need hands-on delivery after the audit.
