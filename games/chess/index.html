How to play

White moves first. Select a piece to see every legal square, then select a target. The engine rejects illegal moves — including moves that leave your own king in check.

  • vs AI: three levels search 1, 2 and 3 plies with alpha-beta pruning, material values and piece-square tables. Every move is capped at 400 ms.
  • 2 players: take turns on the same device. Undo works for both sides.
  • Undo steps back one move locally, or back to your turn against the AI. Castling, en passant and promotion are all recognised.
